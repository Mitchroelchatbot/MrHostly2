FAQ

Good questions, honest answers

Will guests know they're talking to an AI?

That's up to you. Many hosts add a friendly note like "our digital co-host may answer first". Replies are written in your tone, and you can review or edit anything it sends.

What happens with questions it can't answer?

If MrHostly isn't confident, it doesn't improvise. It tells the guest you'll get back to them and pings you with a summary and a suggested reply.

Does it work with Airbnb's rules?

Yes. MrHostly works within each platform's messaging policies and never asks guests to move off-platform.

Can I take over a conversation at any time?

Always. The moment you type in a thread, MrHostly steps back and stays quiet until you hand it back.

Is my data safe?

Your house manuals and guest conversations are encrypted, never sold, and never used to answer other hosts' guests.
